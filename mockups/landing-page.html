The Format

Cricket, adapted for the diamond.

Ball

Hard tennis (red/yellow). CIL supplies the balls for every scheduled match — no need to bring your own.

Ground

Baseball fields across Metro Atlanta. Boundary scoring is set per field category, from a tight 2D-and-out to a full 4-over-the-fence.

Overs

15 a side, 9 players plus a super-sub. No LBWs, no last-man batting, max four overs per bowler.

Schedule

Saturdays and Sundays. Matches can move by mutual agreement between captains — CIL keeps the ground booked either way.
